YOUR_CONSULTING_FORM_GUID The unique ID of your consulting inquiry form HubSpot → Marketing → Forms → Consulting Inquiry → Embed → formId consulting.html
YOUR_CALENDLY_DISCOVERY_URL Your Calendly Discovery Call booking link Calendly → Event Types → Discovery Call → Copy Link consulting.html, index.html
